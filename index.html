📖 Detailed Step-by-Step User Guide

Step 1: Drop Documents or Images Into the Inspector · [Universal Dropzone (#dz)]Drag and drop any `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx`, `.odt`, `.ods`, `.odp`, `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.webp` file onto the inspector dropzone (`#dz`).✓ Result: The engine parses container dictionaries and ZIP XML streams locally.
Step 2: Review Author Names, Company & Revision History · [Metadata Findings Panel (#list)]Review each file card in `#list`. The inspector surfaces PDF `/Info` and `/Metadata` XMP keys, Office `docProps/core.xml` and `app.xml` fields (Creator, LastModifiedBy, Company, Revision count, TotalTime), and image EXIF tags.✓ Result: All hidden identity and revision fields are highlighted clearly on screen.
Step 3: Click Clean & Download All · [Clean All Button (#stripAll)]Click **Clean & download all (`#stripAll`)** (or the per-file **Clean & download** button) to scrub `/Info` dictionaries, XMP packets, and OOXML/ODF `core.xml`/`app.xml` properties.✓ Result: A sanitized copy of your PDF, Office document, or image is rebuilt in memory.
Step 4: Re-Drop to Verify Zero Hidden Fields · [Cleaned Output Verification]Save the exported `.clean.pdf` or `.clean.docx` file and drop it back into `#dz` to confirm all identity and revision fields now read empty.✓ Result: Document is safe to send to clients, opposing counsel, or public portals.

🎛️ UI Controls & Shortcuts Reference

  • File Dropzone (`#dz`) — Accepts PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, ODT, ODS, ODP, JPEG, PNG, and WebP files.
  • Clean & download all (`#stripAll`) — Rewrites metadata dictionaries across all loaded files and triggers local download.
  • Per-File Field Table — Displays exact key/value pairs found inside `/Info`, XMP, `docProps/core.xml`, and `docProps/app.xml`.

💡 Pro Tips & Real-World Workflows

  • Always run Word (`.docx`) and PDF proposals through Metadata Checker before sending to clients—Word records cumulative editing minutes (`TotalTime`) and previous authors by default.
  • After cleaning metadata properties, remember to also check visible document body text for comments or tracked changes.
  • Combine with `share-safe` CLI in CI pipelines if you publish PDFs automatically from a repository.
